Low-Cost Student Nutrition

Healthy Meals for College Students on a Budget

Nutritious eating on campus doesn't require expensive organic grocery hauls or complex culinary equipment. These frameworks prioritize high protein, minimal prep time, and microwave or mini-fridge compatibility.

~$1.75 / Serving

High-Protein Scramble Bowl

Simple microwave or hotplate breakfast ready in under 5 minutes.

  • 3 large eggs or liquid egg whites
  • 1/2 can black beans (rinsed)
  • 2 tbsp salsa & handful of spinach
  • Serve with toasted corn tortilla
~$1.20 / Serving

Overnight Dorm Oats

Zero cooking required; prep in a mason jar or mug before bed.

  • 1/2 cup rolled oats
  • 1/2 cup Greek yogurt (plain)
  • 1/2 cup milk or oat milk
  • Topped with frozen berries
~$2.40 / Serving

Rotisserie Chicken & Rice

The ultimate low-effort, macro-balanced dinner option.

  • 1 cup instant microwave brown rice
  • 4–5 oz shredded rotisserie chicken
  • Steamed frozen broccoli florets
  • Drizzle of sriracha or low-sodium soy sauce
