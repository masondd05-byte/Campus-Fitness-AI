Beginner Workout Plan for College Gym

Stepping into a packed rec center can be intimidating. This 3-day split focuses on versatile dumbbell stations and cable machines so you never have to wait in line for a barbell rack.

Warm-Up & Rest Interval Standards

Begin each training block with 5 minutes of low-incline walking and arm circles. Rest 60 to 90 seconds between dumbbell working sets to manage central fatigue without lingering at stations during peak recreation center volume hours.
