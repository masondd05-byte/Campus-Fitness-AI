Room-Friendly Protocol

Dorm Room Workout Plan

A zero-equipment, high-intensity circuit engineered for tight living quarters. These silent exercises build strength and elevate heart rate without shaking the floor or disturbing roommates.

The 20-Minute Silent Circuit

Perform each movement for 45 seconds with controlled cadence, followed by 15 seconds of transition rest. Complete 3 to 4 rounds total.

1

Elevated Desk or Bed Push-Ups

Place hands on your sturdy desk edge or bed frame. Maintain a rigid plank angle while pressing to isolate chest and triceps without needing wide floor space.

2

3-Second Tempo Air Squats

Descend for three seconds, pause for one second at parallel, and press up smoothly. Eliminates jumping impact while creating high muscle tension.

3

Isometric Doorframe Row

Stand inside your closet or room doorway, grip the molding or frame, and pull against resistance in 10-second isometric bursts to strengthen the upper back.

4

Controlled Glute Bridges

Lie on your back between your rug and wardrobe. Drive through your heels to bridge your hips upward, holding for two seconds at peak contraction.

5

Dead Bug Core Holds

Opposite arm and leg reach from your back without movement in the lower lumbar. Generates deep core stability without crunching or stomping.
